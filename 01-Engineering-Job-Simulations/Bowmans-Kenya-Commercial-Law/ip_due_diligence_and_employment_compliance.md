# Intellectual Property Due Diligence & Employment Compliance Framework

**Client Advisory:** Bowmans Commercial Law Practice  
**Focus:** IP Asset Verification & Statutory Labor Law Compliance  

---

## 1. Intellectual Property (IP) Due Diligence Protocol

During corporate acquisitions, investments, or commercial restructures, an IP due diligence audit mitigates risk by verifying ownership and legal enforceability:

| IP Asset Class | Verification Focus | Risk Mitigation Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Trademarks & Brand Names** | Validity of registration with national IP offices (e.g., KIPI) | Confirm renewal status and ensure no pending infringement challenges. |
| **Proprietary Software / Code** | Ownership assignment from employees and third-party developers | Audit Employment Contracts and Independent Contractor Agreements for IP Assignment Clauses. |
| **Trade Secrets & Know-How** | Adequacy of physical and digital confidentiality safeguards | Verify enforceable Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs) across key staff and partners. |

## 2. Employment Law Advisory & Statutory Compliance

Navigating employment challenges requires balancing corporate operational goals with strict statutory employee protections:

### A. Fair Termination Protocols
* **Substantive & Procedural Fairness:** Ensuring any termination for performance or misconduct meets statutory notice requirements, fair hearing standards, and valid grounds.
* **Redundancy Compliance:** Verifying proper notification to labor authorities, objective selection criteria, and severance pay compliance during corporate downsizing.

### B. Standardized Employment Contracts
* Formulating clear terms regarding probation periods, working hours, non-compete/non-solicitation restrictions, and intellectual property ownership assignment.
